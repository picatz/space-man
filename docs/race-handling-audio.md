# Circuit handling and original audio

All pilots share the deterministic 60 Hz `race.js` model. Kart centers remain inside the nearest course centerline corridor (`width / 2 - 28`); the final 12 units progressively ease the speed limit from 6.4 to 4.2, with a bounded slowdown instead of an abrupt speed clamp. Near the rail, outward throttle is gently guided toward the local road tangent, and non-bouncing contact removes only outward velocity. Inward steering, braking and coasting remain under pilot control. Deliberate reverse driving remains reverse. The three authored widths are 180, 172 and 176 units, shared by physics and rendering. The renderer must keep its visible road aligned with `course().segments` and the authored width. Normal on-road contact can cross the next ordered checkpoint. Inward correction of an already-invalid offcourse pose never counts as checkpoint-driving movement.

Already-invalid offcourse positions move inward at no more than two units per tick. A gradually corrected invalid pose can resume driving as soon as it regains the road. Two seconds continuously outside the road or trapped against the rail under throttle triggers the same last-verified-checkpoint rescue as R: no new gate/lap credit, 25 fuel cost and 1.5-second stationary recovery. Extreme offcourse positions recover immediately. `offroadTicks` is host-only bookkeeping: online guests render existing authoritative position/offroad/recovery fields and do not simulate it. Normal CPU pilots use the same boundaries and retain their steering model.

The optional `race-audio.js` module composes an original Dmaj9 / Bm7 / Gmaj9 / A6 pad, bass and arpeggio bed with soft countdown, checkpoint, boost-pad, rescue, lap and finish cues. It uses native Web Audio only, with no samples, downloads, licenses or runtime dependencies. The engine turbine follows displayed kart speed/boost, without influencing the simulation. Existing global mute, music, music volume and SFX volume settings apply; the race lobby and pause menu expose sound, music and a combined volume control. Battery-saver mode lowers the music voice budget. Reduced-motion remains visual-only and never forces sound on.

Audio creation requires a gesture; online authoritative starts can resume only a context the user already unlocked. Menu/background transitions clear scheduled voices and suspend audio, while return to the runner stops it. Results have a bounded musical resolution. Automated fake-clock tests check voice budgets, cue deduplication and cancellation; real-browser tests check native AudioContext state and node cleanup. These are not a claim of physical-device speaker or listening validation.

Run the normal regression suite with `node --test tests/*.test.cjs`; focused boundary coverage is in `race-handling.test.cjs`, and sound lifecycle coverage is in `race-audio.test.cjs` and the optional Playwright browser suite.
