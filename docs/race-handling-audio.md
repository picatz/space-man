# Circuit handling and original audio

All pilots share deterministic 60 Hz `race.js` rules. Human steering now builds smoothly, reverses within a few ticks, and releases immediately. Natural cruise/boost turning radii are approximately 81/105 units; steering and road assistance share one total yaw budget. The rounded Ember slalom removes its former near-cusp bends. See `human-race-feel.md` for the measurements and acceptance limits.

The visible asphalt edge can be crossed. A slower apron permits kart centers up to `width / 2 + RUNOFF` (`RUNOFF = 48`), while the radius-28 hull stays supported by the existing 88-unit visible apron. The 2D fallback draws matching ground; decorative pylons and arch supports sit outside driveable space. Boost is disabled off asphalt, speed eases down, and the final safety edge converts contact into a bounded slide with a small throttle-only glide. Braking and coasting never receive that glide. Ordered gates use the full legal corridor, but invalid-pose correction and recovery release never grant gate credit.

Already-invalid positions move inward by at most two units per tick; regained ground resumes driving. Two seconds continuously beyond the safety corridor or trapped under throttle triggers the last-verified-checkpoint rescue: no gate/lap credit, 25 fuel cost and a 1.5-second stationary wait. Extreme escapes rescue immediately. Steering smoothing and rescue bookkeeping remain host-only; guests display authoritative snapshots. Race geometry revision 3 rejects incompatible cached roads and props atomically with the asset update.

The optional `race-audio.js` module composes an original Dmaj9 / Bm7 / Gmaj9 / A6 pad, bass and arpeggio bed with soft countdown, checkpoint, boost-pad, rescue, lap and finish cues. It uses native Web Audio only, with no samples, downloads, licenses or runtime dependencies. The engine turbine follows displayed kart speed/boost, without influencing the simulation. Existing global mute, music, music volume and SFX volume settings apply; the race lobby and pause menu expose sound, music and a combined volume control. Battery-saver mode lowers the music voice budget. Reduced-motion remains visual-only and never forces sound on.

Audio creation requires a gesture; online authoritative starts can resume only a context the user already unlocked. Menu/background transitions clear scheduled voices and suspend audio, while return to the runner stops it. Results have a bounded musical resolution. Automated fake-clock tests check voice budgets, cue deduplication and cancellation; real-browser tests check native AudioContext state and node cleanup. These are not a claim of physical-device speaker or listening validation.

Run the normal regression suite with `node --test tests/*.test.cjs`; focused boundary coverage is in `race-handling.test.cjs`, and sound lifecycle coverage is in `race-audio.test.cjs` and the optional Playwright browser suite.
